# Product Backlog

Compact product ideas that are intentionally not part of the active runtime scope.

## Scheduled Mining Sessions

Status: parked for a later launch experiment. No contract change is required.

- Concentrate early users into announced one- or two-hour mining windows instead
  of spreading a small audience across the whole week.
- Show a countdown, active-miner count, occupied tiles, current pool, and the
  current `occupied tiles / 25` chance that a round has a winner.
- Promote sessions through the existing X share flow and community channels.
- Use off-chain badges or event recognition; do not create hidden treasury bets
  or imply guaranteed rewards.
- Measure unique players, occupied tiles, pool size, rounds with winners,
  returning players, Auto-Miner completions, and post-session claims.
- Add only after the normal testnet flow remains stable and there is enough
  community demand to make a coordinated session useful.
